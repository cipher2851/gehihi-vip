# Paper, Thread, and Fold

Before a book becomes a single object, it is a gathering of sheets, folds, thread, board, cloth, and careful alignment.

### What catches the eye?

The grain of paper should usually lie parallel to the spine so pages open without resistance.

### What rewards patience?

A crisp bone-folder crease gives each section an even edge. Visible sewing can be structural and decorative at the same time.

### One last note

A modest handmade binding invites attention to the weight, sound, and movement of pages in the hand.

> An informal guide to bookbinding details, folded paper, and the quiet craft hidden inside a well-made volume.
